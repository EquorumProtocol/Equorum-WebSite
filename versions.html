Frequently Asked Questions

Why was V2 created?

V2 was created to address 22 security vulnerabilities identified during our audit of V1. We take security seriously and wanted to ensure all users have access to a fully secure protocol.

Is V1 still safe to use?

While V1 remains functional, we do not recommend it for new series or investments due to known security issues. Existing V1 series continue to work, but we strongly encourage using V2.

Do I need to migrate from V1 to V2?

Migration is not required but highly recommended. Existing V1 series will continue to function. However, for new investments, we strongly recommend using V2 series.

Will there be a V3?

We are committed to continuous improvement. If new features or security enhancements are needed, we will develop them transparently and communicate clearly with the community.
