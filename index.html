FAQs

Equorum is a DeFi protocol on Arbitrum One that enables protocols to share revenue with their community through tokenized bonds. Instead of diluting token holders, protocols can raise capital by selling future revenue streams as ERC-20 bonds.

Revenue Bonds represent a claim on future protocol revenue, not governance or ownership. They're non-dilutive fundraising instruments that align incentives between protocols and investors through actual cash flows rather than token price speculation.

Yes. All DeFi protocols carry smart contract risk. Additionally, bond holders face revenue risk (if the protocol generates no revenue, distributions are zero) and protocol default risk. Hybrid Series with escrow protection mitigate principal risk but don't eliminate revenue uncertainty.

Soft Bonds rely on protocol reputation and on-chain payment history. Hybrid Bonds include principal protection through escrow—the protocol deposits the full principal amount before selling bonds, guaranteeing investors get their capital back at maturity regardless of protocol performance.
