SIMPLE PROCESS

How it Works

From creation to revenue distribution in 4 steps

1

Protocol Creates Series

Define bond parameters: name, revenue share %, duration, and principal amount to guarantee.

2

Deposit Principal

Protocol deposits principal to escrow. This proves commitment and protects investors.

3

Investors Buy Bonds

Community members purchase bond tokens directly or on DEXs. Funds go to protocol.

4

Revenue Flows

Protocol deposits revenue periodically. Holders claim their share anytime. At maturity, principal returns.
