For Investors: How to Buy Guaranteed Bonds

Complete step-by-step guide on how to purchase revenue bond tokens with principal protection.

Principal Protected
1

Connect Your Wallet

First, you need to connect your Web3 wallet to Equorum. We support MetaMask, Rabby, WalletConnect, and other popular wallets.

Important: Make sure you're connected to Arbitrum One network. Revenue Bonds only work on Arbitrum One mainnet.

💡 Tip: If you don't have a wallet yet, we recommend MetaMask (browser extension) or Rabby Wallet (more advanced users). Both are free and easy to set up.
2

Select a Bond Series

On the Buy Bonds page, select a series from the dropdown. Each guaranteed bond shows:

  • Principal Guarantee - ETH held in escrow, returned at maturity
  • Price per Token - How much ETH per bond token
  • Revenue Share - Percentage of protocol revenue you'll receive
  • Maturity Date - When you can claim your principal back
  • Available Tokens - How many tokens are for sale
Principal Protection: Your investment principal is held in a smart contract escrow and will be returned to you at maturity, regardless of protocol performance.
3

Enter Amount & Buy Tokens

Enter how many tokens you want to buy. The interface shows real-time cost calculation:

  • Token Amount - Number of bond tokens to purchase
  • Token Cost - Price × Amount in ETH
  • Protocol Fee - 2% fee on purchases
  • Total Cost - Final amount you'll pay
Direct Purchase: Unlike DEX trading, you buy directly from the protocol at a fixed price. No slippage, no price impact!
4

Confirm Transaction

After clicking "Buy Tokens", your wallet will prompt you to confirm the transaction:

  1. Review the ETH amount being sent
  2. Check gas fees (typically $0.50-$2 on Arbitrum)
  3. Click "Confirm" in your wallet
  4. Wait for transaction confirmation (~2 seconds on Arbitrum)
  5. Tokens will appear in your wallet!
⚡ Fast & Cheap: On Arbitrum One, transactions confirm in ~2 seconds and cost only $0.50-$2 in gas fees!
5

Claim Revenue & Track Position

After purchase, you'll see your position on the Buy page. You can claim revenue anytime:

  1. Your balance and claimable revenue appear automatically
  2. Click "Claim Revenue" when you have earnings
  3. ETH is sent directly to your wallet
When to Claim: You can claim anytime! Consider gas fees (~$0.50) - wait until claimable amount justifies the gas cost.
6

Claim Principal at Maturity

When the bond matures, you can claim your share of the guaranteed principal:

  • Principal is guaranteed - held in smart contract escrow
  • Proportional share - based on your token balance
  • Tokens are burned - after claiming principal
�️ Guaranteed Return: Your principal is returned regardless of how much revenue the protocol distributed. This is the key benefit of Guaranteed Bonds!
