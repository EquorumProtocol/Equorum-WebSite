For Protocols: How to Create Guaranteed Bonds

Complete step-by-step guide on how to create a revenue bond series with principal protection.

Principal Protected
1

Connect Your Protocol Wallet

Connect the wallet that controls your protocol. This wallet will be the owner of the revenue bond series and will be responsible for distributing revenue.

Important: Use a secure wallet (preferably a multisig like Gnosis Safe) since this wallet will control the series.

🔒 Security Tip: We strongly recommend using a Gnosis Safe multisig wallet for protocol operations. This prevents single points of failure.
2

Fill Bond Parameters

Define the parameters for your guaranteed revenue bond:

  • Token Name: Descriptive name (e.g., "Acme Protocol Bond")
  • Token Symbol: Ticker (e.g., "ACME-RB")
  • Revenue Share %: Percentage of revenue to share (max 50%)
  • Duration: How long the series will run (30 days to 5 years)
  • Total Supply: Number of bond tokens to create
  • Principal Amount: ETH to deposit as guarantee (returned to investors at maturity)
  • Deposit Deadline: Days to deposit principal after creation (max 90 days)
Important: These parameters are PERMANENT and cannot be changed after deployment. Choose carefully!
3

Review & Deploy Contract

Review all parameters carefully before deploying. The deployment process:

  1. Click "Create Series" button
  2. Review the summary modal
  3. Confirm the transaction in your wallet
  4. Wait for transaction confirmation (~2-5 seconds on Arbitrum)
  5. Your series contract is now deployed!
Gas Cost: Deploying a series costs approximately 0.001-0.003 ETH on Arbitrum One (~$2-8). Much cheaper than Ethereum mainnet!
4

Deposit Principal (Critical!)

After deployment, you MUST deposit the principal within the deadline to activate the bond:

  • Principal is locked in escrow until maturity
  • Tokens are minted to your wallet after deposit
  • Failure to deposit = series defaults, reputation damage
Critical: If you don't deposit within the deadline, anyone can declare your series as defaulted. This damages your protocol's reputation permanently!
5

Start Sale & Sell Tokens

After depositing principal, start the token sale so investors can buy directly:

  1. Call startSale(price, treasury) to enable buying
  2. Set token price (e.g., 0.001 ETH per token)
  3. Investors can now buy on the Buy Bonds page
  4. 2% of sales goes to protocol treasury as fee
  5. You can stop sale anytime with stopSale()
� Revenue Stream: 2% of all token sales goes to Equorum protocol. You receive 98% to your treasury address.
6

Deposit Revenue Regularly

The core function! Deposit your protocol's revenue to the series contract regularly:

  • Send ETH to router - Revenue is automatically distributed to holders
  • Holders claim anytime - No lock-up, instant withdrawals
  • Build reputation - Regular deposits improve your on-chain reputation score
  • At maturity - Principal is returned to investors automatically
Transparency: All deposits are on-chain and verifiable on Arbiscan. This builds trust with your community.
7

Series Expiration & Renewal

When your series reaches its end date:

  • You stop depositing revenue to that series
  • Holders can still claim any unclaimed revenue
  • Tokens remain tradeable but have no future revenue rights
  • You can create a NEW series with updated terms
🔄 Renewal Strategy: Many protocols create Series A, B, C with different terms. This allows you to adjust revenue share % and duration based on performance.
