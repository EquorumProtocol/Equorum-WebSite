Revenue Bonds for Investors

Participate in DeFi protocol revenue distribution. Transparent, on-chain, and liquid.

How It Works

Simple 4-step process to participate in revenue sharing

1

Protocol Creates Bond

DeFi protocol creates revenue bond series with fixed terms (revenue share %, duration)

2

You Buy Tokens

Purchase bond tokens at a price set by the protocol (on Uniswap or OTC)

3

Protocol Distributes

Protocol deposits revenue (in ETH) monthly or as agreed

4

You Claim Earnings

Claim your proportional share of revenue anytime. Sell tokens on secondary market if needed.

Key Points for Investors

Always Check ROI

No Lock-up Period

Claim revenue anytime. Sell tokens anytime. Full flexibility.

Growth Potential
