General Questions

Revenue Bonds are tokenized financial instruments that allow DeFi protocols to share revenue with their community. When you hold revenue bond tokens, you automatically receive a proportional share of the protocol's revenue distributions.

Think of it like owning shares in a company that pays dividends, but fully on-chain, transparent, and liquid.

Key differences:

  • Staking: You lock tokens to earn rewards (usually from inflation)
  • Revenue Bonds: You buy tokens to earn real protocol revenue (no lock-up, no inflation)

Revenue bonds distribute ACTUAL revenue from protocol operations (trading fees, NFT sales, etc.), not newly minted tokens.

Revenue Bonds Protocol runs exclusively on Arbitrum One (Ethereum Layer 2).

Why Arbitrum?

  • Low gas fees (~$0.10-$2 per transaction)
  • Fast confirmations (~2-5 seconds)
  • Full Ethereum security
  • Large DeFi ecosystem

The smart contracts are currently undergoing security review. We recommend:

  • Start with small amounts until full audit is complete
  • Review the open-source code on GitHub
  • Check the contract addresses on Arbiscan

💰 For Investors

If a protocol stops distributing revenue, you won't receive further payments. However:

  • You can still sell your tokens on Uniswap
  • You can still claim any unclaimed revenue
  • There's currently no enforcement mechanism to force protocols to pay

Always verify the protocol's reputation and track record before investing!

Main risks:

  • You stop distributing revenue to that series

  • Tokens remain tradeable but have no future revenue rights

⚙️ Technical Questions

We support all Web3 wallets that work with Arbitrum One:

  • MetaMask (most popular)
  • Rabby Wallet (recommended for advanced users)
  • WalletConnect (mobile wallets)
  • Coinbase Wallet
  • Trust Wallet
  • Any wallet with Arbitrum One support

Most wallets have Arbitrum One pre-configured. If not:

  1. Go to Chainlist.org
  2. Search for "Arbitrum One"
  3. Click "Add to MetaMask" (or your wallet)
  4. Approve the network addition

Or visit bridge.arbitrum.io for official instructions.

All smart contracts are open-source and verified on Arbiscan:

  • Factory Contract: View on Arbiscan
  • GitHub Repository: Coming soon

You can read and verify the code yourself before using the protocol.

Typical gas costs on Arbitrum One:

  • Claim revenue: ~$0.10-$0.50
  • Buy/sell tokens on Uniswap: ~$0.50-$2
  • Deploy series (protocols): ~$2-$8
  • Deposit revenue (protocols): ~$0.50-$2

Gas fees vary based on Ethereum mainnet congestion but are always much cheaper than Ethereum L1.
